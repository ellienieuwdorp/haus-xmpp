# Encrypted private groups

Research and implementation snapshot: 2026-09-14. This is a restricted first implementation, not a claim of shipping-client group interoperability.

The client encrypts modern OMEMO messages for every member of a private, nonanonymous MUC, including offline members. It stores exact ciphertext, fingerprint recipients, membership and ratchet advancement together. Changed membership, missing devices, rejected/undecided fingerprints and inaccessible member lists block the entire send. There is no partial-recipient or plaintext fallback.

## Primary specification basis

- [XEP-0384 0.9.1](https://xmpp.org/extensions/xep-0384.html), sections 5.5.1 and 5.8: `urn:xmpp:omemo:2`; complete member/admin/owner union; recipient keys grouped by real bare JID; nonanonymous MUC; encrypted SCE recipient affix for a room. Existing vetted OMEMO primitives and sessions remain unchanged.
- [XEP-0045](https://xmpp.org/extensions/xep-0045.html), sections 9.5, 10.5 and 10.8: membership queries in `http://jabber.org/protocol/muc#admin`; occupant real identities in `http://jabber.org/protocol/muc#user`. A nickname is not a cryptographic identity.
- [XEP-0420](https://xmpp.org/extensions/xep-0420.html): authenticated content in `urn:xmpp:sce:1`. Our first slice requires both sender and room recipient affixes. Requiring the sender affix is stricter than XEP-0384's SHOULD and is an explicit interoperability restriction.
- [XEP-0313](https://xmpp.org/extensions/xep-0313.html): `urn:xmpp:mam:2` results are accepted only inside the active query from the exact archive owner. Room archives and account archives have distinct cursors.
- [XEP-0444](https://xmpp.org/extensions/xep-0444.html): group reaction targets need room-assigned stanza IDs. An encrypted origin ID is not a substitute; the service uses a separate durable room-reference table rather than treating encrypted origin IDs as room assignments.

## Public interfaces and integration

`XMPPClient.refreshGroupRoom` retrieves disco and all three affiliation lists twice. It requires matching results and an unchanged local presence revision. `joinGroupRoom` sends an explicit presence request with room history disabled; `groupPresence` marks confirmed self-presence. Joining does not imply immediate confirmation. Refresh the overview after the presence event.

`XMPPClient.groupRoomSnapshot` reads the last validated local snapshot without IQ traffic; it reports joined only after authenticated self-presence.

Presence can contain multiple `muc#user` items for an occupant connected through several resources. XEP-0045's schema permits this, and the pinned Prosody lab's `build_item_list` emits one item per session. The parser accepts at most 100 consistent entries naming the same bare JID and affiliation, while rejecting mixed identities or affiliations. A live simulator regression exposed this with three valid self items and status 110; the original single-item requirement incorrectly kept the UI waiting to join. Changing the requested nickname invalidates joined state until self-presence confirms the new nickname.

`OMEMOService.knownGroupRooms(account:)` reads durable rooms and memberships. `refreshGroupDevices` returns the live room snapshot and every member's modern devices without approving fingerprints. `cachedGroupDevices` supports offline trust review, including pending historical authors who have since left. Group discovery does not change direct-chat profile selections.

`localIdentity(account:profile:)` reads the existing device ID and fingerprint without publishing or replacing keys. Group identity review uses the modern profile. Device discovery can take multiple network round trips; its result contains the final membership reread, followed by asynchronous persistence. UI consumers must read the current `groupRoomSnapshot` after discovery before updating joined state, because self-presence may arrive after that reread.

Queue a normal durable message intention with `chatID=room`, then call `prepareGroupMessage`. Immediately before submission, call `outgoingGroupEnvelope`; its `PreparedGroupMessage` contains both the saved envelope and expected membership. Pass both to `sendEncryptedGroupMessage`. The transport rechecks membership after acquiring the write gate and again after durable SM journaling. Never submit these envelopes through the direct-message sender. SM resumption refuses group ciphertext; a fresh connection must validate the membership before an explicit outbox retry can send saved bytes.

Receive `encryptedGroupMessage` through `receiveGroup`; on failure, acknowledge only after `hasDurableGroupDisposition` confirms persisted ciphertext/replay or a deliberate nonrecipient discard. Live identity comes from the authenticated room presence mapping. Historical identity comes from the archived `muc#user` real-JID item; today's nickname map cannot authenticate a historical author. Current membership is required for live authors; a separately authenticated archived author may have left, but their cryptographic key still requires approval. Session-confirmation controls go directly to the author's real JID.

`fetchArchivePage(after:limit:room:)` returns `XMPPArchiveMessage.encryptedGroupMessage`. Omit room for the own account archive; pass a known room for its archive. Refresh known rooms before querying the own archive when available. A valid own-archive wrapper may also discover an unknown room, so fresh installations can process previous group traffic. Commit every message or pending ciphertext before its cursor and then handled tokens, exactly as for direct MAM. Malformed outer MAM provenance fails the page. Unsupported or unverifiable inner group content returns an explicit rejection that the synchronizer records before advancing the cursor, bounded to 1,000 metadata-only rejection receipts per account. No author claim or message body is rendered. Addressed unknown-room ciphertext is retained before member discovery; old ciphertext with no key for a new installation has an intentional nonrecipient disposition. Exact ciphertext plus room/sender matching permits own-device echo reconciliation.

## Encrypted reactions and reference provenance

Group reactions use the same encrypted SCE `urn:xmpp:reactions:0` content and real-JID actor binding as text. `queueReactions` dispatches registered rooms to the group policy. `prepareGroupReaction` and `outgoingGroupReactionEnvelope` use the complete membership and fingerprint checks above and return an immutable `PreparedGroupMessage` for the group sender. Direct reaction preparation skips rooms.

The room must advertise `urn:xmpp:sid:0`, including its obligation to replace forged `by=room` assignments. The protocol accepts exactly one room-assigned `<stanza-id>` and never treats a foreign `by`, outer message ID, encrypted origin ID, or MAM cursor alone as that reference. Incoming text authentication, ratchets, ciphertext replay identity and its room reference commit atomically. Exact own ciphertext echoes associate the outgoing message with its room assignment. A later archive copy may fill a missing reference for the exact authenticated ciphertext; changing an existing assignment is rejected.

These references are authenticated by the room service, not end-to-end signatures over the target body. They are stored separately from cryptographically authenticated direct-message origin IDs. A colliding room ID across two messages suppresses association and reaction projection. Full reaction sets replace only the same real author's earlier set; empty sets withdraw, duplicate event IDs preserve the first event, and future timestamps cannot freeze updates. Pending reactions can precede their targets without creating fake message bubbles.

## Deliberate boundaries and remaining release work

- Modern OMEMO only. Legacy groups do not gain the mandatory SCE room binding from the modern specification.
- Existing, members-only, nonanonymous rooms only; at most 100 members and 1,024 recipient devices. No room creation/configuration UI, public/anonymous groups, MIX or invitations yet.
- A server that restricts complete affiliation-list retrieval cannot be used for this group slice. The server offers no atomic membership/list transaction: two consistent reads plus observable-change checks reduce races but cannot promise an impossible atomic server snapshot. A change after an actual socket write cannot retroactively revoke ciphertext.
- Wire-targeted group replies and group-specific media workflows remain work. Group reply quotes must not reuse direct origin IDs as room stanza IDs; display-only quoting remains available. Group reactions and room reference associations have package coverage and native simulator round-trip evidence with the independent test adapter. Shipping-client group reaction compatibility remains unverified.
- Application rejoin, room cursor scheduling, UI trust presentation and encrypted outbox integration have native simulator coverage below. Physical background group delivery and Notification Service Extension group catch-up still require independent verification.
- Run a real Prosody room with at least three members, an offline member, nickname reuse, affiliation changes and a second independent modern client before claiming group interoperability. No such shipping-client group result is claimed here.

## Verification

`GroupChatTests` covers room feature/affiliation validation, newly added member presence without membership promotion, self departure, nickname spoofing, conflicting real JIDs, room-stanza-ID authority/collisions and archive-owner/historical-author binding. `GroupServiceTests` covers encrypted room-to-DM substitution rejection without ratchet loss, multi-recipient encryption, pending trust discovery/retry, archived former-member delivery, replay, direct session confirmations, missing offline devices, rejected keys and immutable ciphertext after membership changes, group reaction full-set updates/withdrawal, replay and no extra bubbles. `GroupReferenceTests` verifies schema8 reference separation, exact-echo assignment, atomic group reaction persistence and ambiguous-target suppression.

## Disposable live adapter

`Scripts/Interop/group_peer.py` runs only generated `omemo-*` accounts and rooms on the localhost lab. It uses the unchanged upstream ratchet/payload libraries plus a test-only SCE adapter, validates the complete affiliation union and real occupant JIDs, and echoes encrypted text/reaction sets. The room's member list is normalized into Slixmpp `JID` objects: the current upstream affiliation helper returns strings despite its annotation. Generic callback failure types/stack locations are logged without ciphertext, keys or message contents. This is independent adapter evidence, not a claim that a shipping third-party client supports the entire feature.

On 2026-09-14, the final native simulator group test passed in 107.585 seconds. It joined the generated two-member room, explicitly accepted each fingerprint, asserted an encrypted text echo and the incoming author's real JID, sent an encrypted reaction and asserted both authors' reactions without extra bubbles, then reconnected and verified archive deduplication, rejoin and another encrypted exchange. The adapter independently decrypted the text and reaction; its echo acknowledgements were 20:57:32.879419 UTC (text), 20:57:44.765763 UTC (reaction) and 20:58:07.639503 UTC (post-reconnect text). Ignored artifacts are `work/group-ui/ninth.xcresult`, `work/group-ui/ninth.log` and `work/omemo-groups/peer-third.log`. A separate native public-API probe confirmed unjoined state immediately after the join request, followed by joined state on self-presence, and left the room cleanly.

The latest relevant package suites passed: 47 XMPPProtocol tests, 46 OMEMO tests and 40 MessageStore tests. The peer's string-to-JID regression also passed. These checks establish the implemented restricted workflow; they do not establish multi-server or shipping-client interoperability, three-member live delivery, or physical background group notifications.
